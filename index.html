QUESTIONS

Frequently asked.

If your question is not here, send a WhatsApp message and describe what you need help with.

Ask a question →
Do you come to my property and do the work?

No. True North is a consulting and guidance service. We do not perform handyman work or carry out repairs.

Can you tell me exactly which contractor to use?

We can help you understand what type of professional may be appropriate and what questions to ask, but we do not sell contractor services or guarantee the work of third parties.

Can you review a contractor quote?

Yes. We can help you understand the proposed scope, materials, assumptions and questions you may want to raise before accepting a quote. We do not issue contractor quotes ourselves.

Can you tell me what materials to buy?

We can discuss suitable materials, quantities and considerations based on the information available. Final purchasing decisions remain yours.

What does membership include?

Membership is designed for homeowners who want ongoing access to guidance. It includes one Quick Advice consultation each month, ongoing WhatsApp questions, priority booking and 50% off Project Consultations and Project Planning.

Is the advice a guarantee that my project will work?

No. Consulting is based on the information you provide and is intended to help you make informed decisions. Complex, regulated or safety-critical work should be assessed by an appropriately qualified professional.
