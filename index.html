QUESTIONS

Frequently asked.

If your question is not here, send a WhatsApp message and describe what you need help with.

Ask a question →
Do I need to know exactly what is wrong?

No. You can start with a description, photos or a video of the problem. We can help work out what needs attention.

Can you help me decide whether to DIY or hire someone?

Yes. We can look at the job, explain the likely requirements and help you decide what is realistic for your skills, time and budget.

Can you review a contractor quote?

Yes. We can help you understand the scope of work, materials and the assumptions behind a quote so you can make a more informed decision.

Do you supply materials?

Material requirements can be discussed as part of the job. Where appropriate, we can help you understand what to buy and what alternatives may be suitable.
